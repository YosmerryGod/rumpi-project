# RUMPY Brand Guidelines

## Core identity

RUMPY is a place for people to meet and communicate. The identity should feel direct, friendly, modern, and unmistakable at small sizes.

## Logo

The core symbol is a single white speech bubble containing three dots. The dots represent an open conversation; the speech bubble makes the communication purpose immediately recognizable.

- Use the supplied SVG as the master artwork.
- Keep the icon square and preserve its rounded-square background.
- Do not stretch, rotate, add shadows, or add outlines.
- Do not place text inside the app icon.
- Keep clear space around the symbol when used outside the app-icon container.

## Color palette

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Merah Nusantara | `#D92738` | Primary brand and app-icon background |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Logo symbol and light surfaces |
| Ink | `#191919` | Primary text and dark surfaces |

The core logo is intentionally flat. Avoid gradients in the master brand mark.

**Where conversations meet.**
